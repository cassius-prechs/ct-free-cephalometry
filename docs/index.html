Toward CT-Free 3D Cephalometric Landmark Estimation Using MediaPipe Face Landmarker

1Keio University, Yokohama, Japan, 2Tokyo Dental College, Tokyo, Japan

Estimating 24 cephalometric landmarks from only 400 sparse MediaPipe Face Landmarker points — no CT scan of the patient required at inference time.

Abstract

In orthodontic diagnosis, 3D cephalometric analysis from CT provides more reliable measurements than 2D radiography, but the associated radiation exposure limits repeated imaging of growing patients. We propose a pipeline that estimates 24 cephalometric landmarks from only 400 sparse 3D facial points produced by MediaPipe Face Landmarker, without requiring a CT scan of the patient at inference time, combining cross-attention coarse-to-fine estimation, a statistical shape prior, and a complementary Ridge regression ensemble. Because paired real-photograph/CT data at this scale are unavailable, our main evaluation uses MediaPipe landmarks extracted from CT-derived STL renders; on 40 held-out cases from a 240-case jaw-deformity cohort, the pipeline outperforms PointNet and MLP baselines on every metric (4.36 mm MRE) and reaches an ANB angle error of 1.71° with 77.5% skeletal classification accuracy—a gap to a CT-based reference far smaller in clinical classification than in positional accuracy, indicating much of the clinically decisive information survives the removal of CT. A pilot study on real clinical photographs, however, reveals a substantially larger sim-to-real domain gap, indicating that transfer to real photographs remains an open problem before clinical deployment.
